FAQ

Who founded CyberSec Mastery?

CyberSec Mastery was founded by Roshab Bhandari, a self-taught security practitioner who built the platform from his own study notes, labs, and research.

What is CyberSec Mastery?

An independent cybersecurity education platform offering structured, hands-on learning roadmaps, labs, and blog write-ups across networking, Linux, Python, ethical hacking, web security, cloud security, and digital forensics.

Is CyberSec Mastery free?

The core roadmap, blog articles, and learning resources are free to access. Any future premium labs or cohort programs will be clearly marked.

Who is Roshab Bhandari?

The founder and creator of CyberSec Mastery — a self-taught practitioner with a background in networking, Linux, and programming, building the platform he wishes had existed when he started.

How can I learn cybersecurity?

Start with the Learning Roadmap, which sequences networking, Linux, and Python fundamentals before moving into ethical hacking, web security, cloud security, and digital forensics through hands-on labs.
